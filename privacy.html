Privacy Policy

MonLook: Virtual Makeup

Support Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

MonLook ("the app") is a virtual makeup app made by Ahmed Mansour (publisher) and Amr Elkhayat (developer) ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and what happens to it.

The short version: current versions process your camera, face and selected photos on your iPhone and do not upload them to us. There are no accounts, analytics SDKs or ads. You can choose to export an image through Share. Earlier beta versions used analytics as described in section 4.

Which versions this policy covers

Version 1.0 build 5 removed TelemetryDeck. The no-analytics description applies to that build and subsequent versions covered by this policy. Earlier installed beta builds, including 1.0 build 4, have different analytics behaviour. Updating this policy does not change an older installed build or erase data it already transmitted.

1. Camera and face data

  • The app uses your front camera to show makeup on your face in real time. You choose whether to allow camera access; if you don't, you can still try makeup on the built-in model faces.
  • To place makeup correctly, the app finds the position of your eyes, brows, lips and face outline in each camera frame using Apple's on-device Vision framework. It also measures the brightness of your face so makeup follows the light, and, when you tap Match my skin, the average colour of your cheeks and forehead.
  • All of this happens only on your iPhone. Camera images, face positions, skin tone and brightness measurements are not sent to us or to anyone else, are not stored after the camera frame is drawn, and are never used for identification, advertising or any purpose other than showing makeup to you.
  • The optional Face tracking test uses Apple's ARKit on supported iPhones to check face tracking. It also runs entirely on your device and stores nothing except the fact that you finished the test.

2. Photos

  • Choose photo lets you pick one portrait from your photo library using Apple's photo picker. The app only receives the photo you choose; it cannot see the rest of your library and does not ask for photo library permission.
  • When you save a look, the app stores a Before and an After photo and the makeup settings on your iPhone, inside the app. They are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting a look in the app removes it; deleting the app removes all of them. Like other app data, they may be included in your own iPhone or iCloud backups, which are controlled by your Apple account, not by us.
  • Share creates a Before and After image and opens the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app or contact there, and what happens next is governed by the app you share to.

3. Saved settings

The app stores on your iPhone: your saved presets, your last used mode (Camera or Model), how many looks you have saved (to decide when to ask for a rating), and whether you finished the face tracking test. This stays on your device.

4. Analytics and older beta versions

Current versions, starting with 1.0 build 5: the app contains no analytics, third-party crash-reporting or advertising SDK and does not send usage statistics or analytics device identifiers. If you choose to share crash reports with developers in your iPhone's settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may provide crash reports under its own services and your sharing choices.

Earlier beta versions, including 1.0 build 4: those versions used TelemetryDeck to send feature-use and session events, sometimes including Camera or Model mode, a ready-made look, a selected foundation shade or a sharing destination. The SDK also sent basic technical information such as device model, operating system, app version, language and region, and a hashed identifier to distinguish usage. Those events did not contain camera images, selected or saved photos, facial landmarks, face meshes or raw skin measurements. TelemetryDeck describes its processing and anonymisation at https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/guides/privacy-faq/.

Those earlier versions do not have an in-app analytics switch. To stop further analytics from one of those versions, update to a version without TelemetryDeck or stop using the older build. An update does not delete events already sent. Contact monlook.app@outlook.com with privacy questions or requests about earlier usage data; do not send facial photos to identify an analytics record.

5. Ratings

After you have saved a couple of looks, the app may ask iOS to show Apple's standard rating prompt. Ratings and reviews are handled by Apple; we only see them on the App Store like everyone else.

6. What the current version does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics or usage statistics.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers (we do not ask for tracking permission because we do not track you).
  • No selling or renting of data.
  • No uploading of your face, camera images or photos.

7. Children

MonLook is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The current app version does not send usage analytics or upload facial photos. The older-version distinction in section 4 also applies here.

8. Your choices

  • You can turn camera access on or off at any time in Settings → MonLook.
  • You can delete any saved look or preset in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your iPhone.
  • Current versions do not send app analytics. If you used an earlier beta, see section 4. If you email us, your address and the information you include are processed to handle your request. Contact us about privacy requests or earlier usage data; do not include facial photos or other sensitive information unless needed for your request.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, mention them in the app's update notes.

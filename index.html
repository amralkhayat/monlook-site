Frequently asked questions

The camera is black or doesn't open

MonLook needs camera access to show makeup on your face. Go to Settings → MonLook and turn on Camera. Without camera access you can still try makeup on the built-in model faces.

The makeup doesn't sit on my face correctly

Face the camera in good, even light and keep your whole face in view. Glasses, hair over the eyes or very dark rooms can make tracking less accurate. Moving your head slowly helps the makeup follow you.

"Match my skin" picked the wrong shade

Try again facing a window or soft daylight, without strong coloured light or shadows on your face. You can always choose a different shade by hand.

Where are my saved looks?

Saved looks are stored inside the app on your iPhone. Tap the photos button at the top of the screen to see them. Your device or iCloud backup may include this app data. Deleting the app removes the local copies; it does not erase backups or images you previously exported.

Do you upload my face or photos?

MonLook processes your camera images, face landmarks and selected photos on your iPhone and does not upload them to us. If you choose Share, an image is exported to the app or contact you select. Saved app data may also be included in your own device or iCloud backups. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Do you collect any data?

Current versions, starting with 1.0 build 5, have no accounts, analytics SDKs or ads and do not send usage statistics or analytics device identifiers. Earlier beta versions, including 1.0 build 4, used TelemetryDeck. The Privacy Policy explains those earlier versions and Apple's optional crash-report sharing.

Website visits and support emails are separate from the app's analytics: see website hosting and support email for the information used when you open this site or contact us.

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone running iOS 17 or later with a front camera.

Frequently asked questions

The camera is black or doesn't open

MonLook needs camera access to show makeup on your face. Go to Settings → MonLook and turn on Camera. Without camera access you can still try makeup on the built-in model faces.

The makeup doesn't sit on my face correctly

Face the camera in good, even light and keep your whole face in view. Glasses, hair over the eyes or very dark rooms can make tracking less accurate. Moving your head slowly helps the makeup follow you.

"Match my skin" picked the wrong shade

Try again facing a window or soft daylight, without strong coloured light or shadows on your face. You can always choose a different shade by hand.

Where are my saved looks?

Saved looks are kept only on your iPhone, inside the app. Tap the photos button at the top of the screen to see them. Deleting the app also deletes your saved looks.

Do you upload my face or photos?

No. All face tracking and makeup happen on your iPhone. Your camera, face and photos never leave your device. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone running iOS 17 or later with a front camera.
